<img class="about-photo" src="{{ '/assets/images/about/profile-photo.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Christol Jones holding a GhanaCodes certificate">Aspiring Biomedical Engineer driven to close the diagnostic gap between rural communities and centralized hospitals. My journey began with co-developing a solar-powered health kiosk, where I realized that tracking basic vital signs cannot detect deep-tissue diseases like pneumonia or maternal complications. This experience exposed the sharp geographic divide in healthcare access, inspiring my mission to major in Biomedical Engineering. I want to build the future of decentralized medicine by integrating portable, low-cost imaging devices with secure, edge-computed AI, safely bringing advanced diagnostic power directly to the frontlines of community health.
