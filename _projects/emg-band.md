The ƆDADEƐ EMG Band is a concept for a wearable wristband designed for people with upper-limb amputations. Its goal is to bridge the gap between users and computer systems, so they can take part more fully in education, work, and social life.

## The problem

- People with upper-limb amputations can face barriers in a digital world, including difficulty using everyday devices.
- Advanced prosthetic limbs are expensive and can require extensive training.
- Voice recognition and eye-tracking are useful, but can fall short on complex tasks.
- In areas like Ghana, access to these advanced solutions is often limited.

## The idea

- **Control:** EMG sensors convert muscle signals into digital commands.
- **Connection:** Bluetooth makes the band compatible across devices.
- **Daily use:** an adjustable fit and an intuitive interface are meant to support long wear and a short learning time.
- **Access:** the band is proposed as a cost-effective alternative to advanced prosthetics.

## Where else it could go

The team also explored using muscle-signal control in gaming and esports, virtual and augmented reality, and robotics, including control of robotic arms and drones.
