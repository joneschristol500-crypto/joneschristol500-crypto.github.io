HealthPort is a solar-powered, AI-enabled healthcare kiosk built to accelerate patient-physician appointment scheduling in underserved rural communities. It addresses gaps in late diagnosis and limited access to healthcare services.

The kiosk integrates:

- Multi-parameter vital monitoring
- AI-based risk stratification
- Secure digital records
- Remote clinician handoff, for scalable, off-grid deployment

HealthPort won the Energy Commission Senior High School Renewable Energy Challenge, a first in the school's history, and earned national media coverage in The Ghanaian Times for advancing sustainable, technology-driven healthcare access across rural areas, CHPS compounds, and emergency settings.
