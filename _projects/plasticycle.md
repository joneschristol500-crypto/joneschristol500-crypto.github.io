As Vice President and Campaign Manager of this student-led recycling club, I worked to address plastic-derived carcinogens, educating students on how open burning, improper disposal, and microplastic exposure increase cancer risk.

## The campaign

- Led a school-wide plastic reduction campaign that achieved a 40% decrease in on-campus plastic waste.
- Converted recycled materials into $1,000 in program funding, and reinvested the proceeds to deploy 30 recycling bins.
- Organized an industrial site visit to Safisana Ghana Limited to study waste management practices that reduce carcinogenic emissions.
- Earned Honorable Mention among 5,700 global participants in the International Slingshot Challenge, from the National Geographic Society and the Paul G. Allen Family Foundation, for advancing community-level cancer risk reduction.
