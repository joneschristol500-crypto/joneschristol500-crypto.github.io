**Research Intern, ThinkNeuro, LLC** · Neuroimaging Techniques & Analysis · June 2026 – August 2026

As part of a 10-member research team, I analyzed neuroimaging publication and citation data using R and RStudio. The work covered bibliometric research to identify trends, influential studies, and emerging techniques, along with R-based visualizations and bibliometric maps used to develop a research abstract.

I completed two ThinkNeuro programs in August 2026:

- [R Programming & Bibliometric Methods, with Distinction]({{ '/assets/images/certificates/thinkneuro-r-distinction.jpg' | relative_url }}) (30 hours). The distinction was awarded for exceptional performance on the final technical examination.
- [SRIP Intensive in Neuroscience Bibliometrics]({{ '/assets/images/certificates/thinkneuro-srip.jpg' | relative_url }}) (100 hours).

## Research poster

**Multimodal Neuroimaging for Presurgical Localization in Pediatric Drug-Resistant Epilepsy: A Bibliometric Analysis of the 100 Most-Cited Studies**

Azaan Asad, Farhat Ahmed, Dhriti Neogi, Christol Jones, Snigdha Anchoori, Elizabeth Chai, Alejandro Rojas, Theodore Lim, Sofia Melendez-Santiago, Aneesh Balaji

*Research question: How can multimodal neuroimaging enhance presurgical localization and influence postoperative seizure outcomes in children with drug-resistant epilepsy?*

<div class="stat-row">
  <div><strong>32/100</strong> studies used two or more presurgical modalities</div>
  <div><strong>30/32</strong> reported improved or additional localization</div>
  <div><strong>15/32</strong> reported changed or guided surgical planning</div>
  <div><strong>22/25</strong> studies testing the link found a favorable outcome association</div>
</div>
